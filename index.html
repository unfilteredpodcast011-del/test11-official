About The Product

What is Test 11?

Test 11 is an Android test-preparation mobile application engineered specifically for competitive civil service candidates preparing for the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC Civil Services Prelims) and the Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission (TNPSC Group 1, 2, 4) exams.

Timed Exam Simulations

Experience the actual pressure of the examination hall with strictly timed papers (e.g. 25 questions in 30 minutes, or 50 questions in 60 minutes) with auto-submission upon expiry.

Multilingual Test Delivery

Every question and multiple-choice answer option is thoroughly translated and available in English, Tamil (தமிழ் - TNPSC focused), and Hindi (हिन्दी).

Instant PDF Diagnostic Reports

Upon test submission, candidates immediately download a branded official performance report listing accuracy, correct answers, negative marks, and question explanations.

What Users Receive After Unlocking:

  • Full access to the selected timed mock exam questions
  • Server-side answer validation and instant scoring
  • Detailed answer explanation key for every question
  • Downloadable official PDF analysis report
