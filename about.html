About Test 11

Empowering Competitive Exam Aspirants Across India

Test 11 is an independent exam practice platform designed to help serious aspirants prepare for high-stakes government competitive exams through timed evaluations.

1. What is Test 11?

Test 11 is an Android educational application built specifically to address the daily practice needs of candidates preparing for competitive civil service exams. Rather than imposing hefty annual bundles or recurring coaching subscriptions, Test 11 operates on an accessible pay-per-test model (₹1.00 per attempt), making high-quality mock testing available to all aspirants regardless of their geographic or financial background.

2. Who is it For?

Test 11 is tailored for candidates appearing for:

  • Union Public Service Commission (UPSC Civil Services Prelims): General Studies Paper 1 coverage including Indian History, Polity, Geography, Economy, Environment, and General Science.
  • Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission (TNPSC Group 1, Group 2, Group 2A, and Group 4): Comprehensive General Studies and Aptitude syllabus curated to state exam standards.

3. What the App Offers

Strict Exam Timer Simulation

Real-time countdown timer with auto-submission upon expiry, simulating real examination hall conditions.

Full Multilingual Questions

Questions, options, and explanations available in English, Tamil (தமிழ்), and Hindi (हिन्दी) with seamless dynamic language switching.

Instant Diagnostic Scorecards

Server-verified score calculation, accuracy percentage, time taken per question, and negative marking penalty calculation.

Downloadable PDF Reports

Official branded performance PDF report containing complete answer keys and explanations saved directly to your device.
