Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of Laven Lokesh Barani ("we", "our", or "us") on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the Test 11 mobile application ("App") or our website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information necessary to deliver and evaluate your test simulations:

  • Account Information: Full Name, Email Address, Contact Phone Number, and District (provided during student profile registration).
  • Academic Preparation Details: Target exam selected (UPSC or TNPSC), preferred language preference (English, Tamil, or Hindi), and years of preparation.
  • Test & Performance Data: Questions attempted, answers selected, time taken per question, scorecards, and diagnostic report history.
  • Payment Transaction Records: Order ID, Payment ID, amount paid (₹1), timestamp, and payment status (Success, Failed, Cancelled) generated by Razorpay. Note: We DO NOT collect, store, or have access to your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords.

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used solely to:

  • Provide authentic timed test evaluations and calculate correct/incorrect answers.
  • Generate downloadable PDF performance scorecards with candidate details.
  • Maintain the accuracy of competitive leaderboards and streak statistics.
  • Maintain your payment and transaction audit records for unlocked tests.
  • Respond to user support inquiries and resolve technical issues.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We engage trusted third-party services that process data in compliance with strict privacy standards:

  • Razorpay: Payment gateway facilitating secure transactions. Razorpay processes your payment information subject to their own Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Secure cloud database hosting user profiles, questions, and test scores.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or needed to provide services. We implement industry-standard organizational and technical measures to protect candidate records against unauthorized access.
