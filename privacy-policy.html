Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Laven Lokesh Barani ("we", "our", or "us") collects, protects, uses, and handles candidate information through the Test 11 mobile application ("App") and website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to facilitate timed mock tests and diagnostic performance tracking:

  • Profile Data: Candidate Name, Email Address, and Contact Phone Number (collected during registration to personalize your scorecards).
  • Academic Preparation Preferences: Chosen target exam stream (UPSC Civil Services or TNPSC) and selected interface language (English, Tamil, or Hindi).
  • Test & Performance Results: Question attempts, answers marked, timer values, overall scores, and PDF diagnostic scorecards.
  • Payment Transaction Identifiers: Razorpay Order ID, Razorpay Payment ID, payment timestamp, and transaction status (Success, Failed, Refunded).
Card & Banking Security: Test 11 NEVER collects, processes, or stores your debit/credit card numbers, CVV codes, netbanking passwords, or UPI PINs. All financial transactions are handled directly through Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway.

3. How We Use Collected Data

The information we collect is strictly used to:

  • Authenticate your profile and unlock tests purchased via ₹1 Razorpay payments.
  • Evaluate test answers, apply negative marking rules, and generate performance analytics.
  • Compile downloadable official PDF diagnostic reports.
  • Maintain candidate test attempt history and streak statistics.
  • Deliver support assistance and investigate transaction queries.

4. Data Deletion Rights

Candidates have the right to request the complete deletion of their account, personal profile, and test performance history at any time.

To request data deletion, send an email to plantgreeninertiamanagement@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Data Deletion Request". All associated profile records and attempt histories will be permanently purged within 7 working days.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on trusted third parties strictly to support core app functionality:

  • Razorpay: Secure payment gateway facilitating ₹1 in-app test unlock transactions.
  • Supabase: Secure database infrastructure hosting test questions, scores, and support inquiries.

6. Shipping & Delivery Policy

Digital Service: Test access is unlocked instantly after successful payment. No physical shipping.
