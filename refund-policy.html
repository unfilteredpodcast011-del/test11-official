1. Nature of Digital Goods

In Test 11, purchases consist of immediate digital access to timed examination questions, server-verified scoring algorithms, and proprietary answer keys. Since access to digital content is granted instantly upon confirmation of payment:

  • Once a test has been unlocked and attempted, payments are generally non-refundable.
  • Users can cancel an initiated payment at any time before authorization in the Razorpay checkout modal without any charge being incurred.

2. Failed Transactions & Double Charges

In scenarios where:

  • An amount of ₹1 is debited from your bank account or UPI app, but the transaction fails or the test remains locked due to network interruptions.
  • Multiple deductions occur accidentally for the same test attempt.

The debited amount is typically reversed automatically by your banking institution or Razorpay within 5 to 7 working days. If the refund is not reflected, please contact our support desk with your Razorpay Payment ID and date of transaction.

3. Cancellation Policy

Because services on Test 11 are billed on a one-time per-test basis (₹1 per unlock) and there are no recurring monthly or annual auto-renewal subscriptions, there are no ongoing recurring subscriptions to cancel.
