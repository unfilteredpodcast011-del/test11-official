Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

1. Nature of Digital Goods

On Test 11, purchases consist of immediate digital access to timed examination question sets, server-side answer evaluation algorithms, and proprietary solution keys. Since access to digital content is granted instantly upon confirmation of payment:

  • Payments are non-refundable after the test starts: Once a candidate unlocks and begins attempting a test, the digital service is considered fully delivered and the payment cannot be refunded.
  • Before completing payment authorization within the Razorpay checkout modal, candidates may cancel the transaction at any moment without any deduction.

2. Failed Transactions & Auto-Refunds

If an amount of ₹1.00 is deducted from your bank account, card, or UPI app, but the test fails to unlock due to network interruption or server delay:

  • The debited amount is automatically reversed by your banking network or Razorpay to your original payment source within 5 to 7 working days.
  • If an accidental double deduction occurs for the same test attempt, the duplicate amount is likewise automatically reversed within 5 to 7 working days.

4. Cancellation Policy

Because services on Test 11 are billed on a one-time per-test basis (₹1.00 per attempt) and there are no recurring monthly, quarterly, or annual auto-renewal subscriptions, there are no ongoing memberships to cancel.
